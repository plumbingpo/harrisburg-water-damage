Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Harrisburg Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Harrisburg, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. Because of it, cleanup follows the toughest rules in the trade, which means full protective gear, barriers to contain the area, tearing out any porous material that met sewage and disinfecting all that is left. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

The cleanup process involves removing all standing sewage, extracting contaminated water from structural materials, removing and properly disposing of every porous material that contacted sewage (carpet, padding, drywall, insulation), thoroughly disinfecting hard surfaces, applying antimicrobial treatments and completing structural drying to prevent secondary mold growth. We also deodorize the affected area with industrial-grade hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging equipment to eliminate lingering sewage odors.

Why Professional Sewage Cleanup Matters

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • In an emergency a plumber needs the cleanout, so learn where yours is and keep it in good shape
  • Fit a strainer over each sink and shower drain so hair and debris stay out of the pipes
  • Keep cooking oil, grease and food scraps out of the drain, where they build into clogs
  • Stormwater from a sump or downspout should never drain into the sewer, since the extra volume invites a backup
  • Flush nothing but toilet paper, because wipes, even 'flushable' ones, cause clogs
  • Install a backwater valve on your sewer connection to prevent backups from the municipal system

When to Call for Sewage Cleanup in Harrisburg

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Running a sink or washer and hearing a toilet or nearby drain gurgle
  • A cleanout that weeps sewage, or wet foul patches in the lawn along the sewer path
  • A toilet overflow containing waste that has spread onto flooring or carpet
  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs
  • Sluggish drainage at all fixtures, a sign the main line is blocking up
  • Sewage residue visible around drain openings or on basement floors
  • Strong sewage or rotten egg odors coming from drains or lower levels
